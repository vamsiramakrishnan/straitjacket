# Optimize output delivery

Evolve `choose_emission(state, options) -> option_id`. Small results that add no
derived evidence should pass through without scaffolding. Floods require an
address, and failures require their complete typed census and decisive fact.
Starvation may densify. Preserve required facts before minimizing visible
tokens and downstream turns. Change only the `EVOLVE-BLOCK`.

Each option exposes its capabilities and dollars, visible tokens, model turns,
tool calls, and latency; do not infer completion from price.
