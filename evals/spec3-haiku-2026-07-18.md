## Read of the table

1. **Quality is arm-independent here.** 16/16 held-out acceptance tests
   passed in every arm; Opus scores 7–10 with no wrapper advantage. On
   tasks this size, haiku completes the spec regardless of harness — the
   wrappers are competing on *economics only*.
2. **Cache doctrine confirmed again**: sj posts the best hit rates
   (96.3/98.3%), headroom the worst (83.9/90.1%) — per-request rewriting
   costs hits, append-only digests protect them. Consistent with the
   overhaul-3arm measurement (80.6–84.2% vs 96.5–98.1%).
3. **But sj lost this benchmark**: 33 turns (cap) on both tasks, 2.7–3.8×
   naive's cost, worst wall-clock. On small spec-driven *creation* tasks
   with a lean model, harness friction exceeded containment benefit.
   Publishing the loss with the diagnosis below — that is what the
   benchmark is for.

## Diagnosis (from the sj transcripts, receipts inline)

Zero guard denials — steering was not the friction. The burn is one loop:
haiku ran `pytest -v` (199 lines), the emission gate returned a correct
`pytest/v1` digest — `tests: 29 · passed 21 · failed 8 · first failure
[detail + span]` — and haiku, wanting all 8 failures, **re-ran the suite
eight times with manual slicers** (`| head -100`, `| tail -50`,
`--tb=line`, `-x`, `| grep`) instead of resolving the digest's span or
narrowing with `ctx get`. The piped re-runs then broke the pytest profile
("summary line not found"), making each digest worse than the last.

Root cause, in the taxonomy's own terms: **the digest showed one failure
when the task needed the failure *census*.** This is precisely the
hypothesis-reversal the rtk corpus taught for lint ("diagnostics needed
structure, not compression" → `lint/v1` exact censuses) — applied to lint
but never to pytest. A lean model with no skill tier (bare `ctx wrap`
injects one discipline line; the Antigravity skill never loads here) has
no retrieval vocabulary, so an incomplete census converts directly into
re-execution.

## Fix candidates (ranked, measurable — next wave's referees are ready)

1. **`pytest/v1` failing-test census**: enumerate every failing test name
   with a span each (the lint/v1 lesson applied to pytest). Success
   metric: sj turns on this exact benchmark drop toward naive's; the
   re-run loop disappears from transcripts.
2. **Retrieval teaching in the wrap tier**: the discipline line (or the
   emission-gate digest itself, for lean models) must teach the two-verb
   recovery vocabulary (`ctx get run:<id> --span <s>`, `ctx search
   run:<id>`) — hint follow-through is LADDERS edge 7, now with a
   measured failure attached.
3. **Re-run detection**: the hook sees the same test command re-issued
   with slicing pipes within a session — that is skill rule 8 ("never
   re-run to recover output") violated mechanically; an
   opportunity-ledger entry + remediation pointing at the existing
   handle would make the anti-pattern both taught and counted.

## Addendum: transcript audit of the three open questions

**What does headroom have that won here? Nothing — it stayed out of the
way.** Its tokenbucket transcript shows 7 tool calls: read spec, glob,
three Writes, ONE pytest run (which passed first try — haiku one-shotted
the implementation), a smoke-check. At these payload sizes (≤10 KiB) its
compressors idle and it behaves as a passthrough (with proxy-churn cache
cost: 83.9–90.1% hits). Its 8-turn cell is single-seed luck, not
mechanism; on csvq it was naive-parity (14 vs 11 turns). The honest
comparative: **headroom's null intervention beat our active one** on this
regime.

**Why are we so different from naive? The rewrite steering digested
cheap, batch-valuable information.** Every pytest ran through the birth
gate by design (auto-steering rewrite), so naive-haiku saw 2,439 tokens
of raw output with all 8 failures and batch-fixed them, while sj-haiku
saw a ~300-token digest with one failure. The containment saved ~2k
tokens per view and spent 20+ extra turns — the failure-budget doubling
fired correctly but `pytest/v1`'s structure has nothing to spend the
extra room on except the first failure's traceback.

**What happened to the conditional systems? They all fired to spec — on
the wrong axis.** Audit: engagement did NOT strip hints (initial
hypothesis wrong — all 16 digests carried `next:` lines and spans);
failure asymmetry fired (budget ×2, wasted by profile structure); window
pressure and adoption steering correctly dormant. The measured scandal is
**hint follow-through: 16 emitted, 0 followed** (LADDERS edge 7, now
with receipts), and the same pytest command re-issued 8× with slicing
pipes — mechanically detectable, counted by nothing. Every ladder we own
modulates budget sizes or steering strength (the flood axis); none
modulates digest *structure* or reacts to behavioral evidence of
information starvation (the re-run loop). The failure lived entirely on
that unpopulated axis — including the inversion it implies: for lean
models, *denser inline censuses beat retrieval affordances*, the exact
opposite of the current lean-model policy of trimming suggestions.

**Gate verdict: partial — improved, not passed.** No more turn-cap hits;
sj turns −24/−36%, cost −30/−38% round-over-round; holdout 16/16 held;
cache hit still best (98.0/98.1%). But sj sits at **1.9× naive turns on
both tasks** — above the ≤1.5× gate. (Naive's own numbers moved with the
seed — 26→13 on tokenbucket — so the ratio uses same-round naive, as the
gate specifies.)

**The reflex fired, with receipts** — and the receipts expose the v2
refinement. The sj outcome ledgers show starvation→densify events: 6 on
tokenbucket, 2 on csvq. Not the "fires once" ideal — and reading them
against the session shape says why: the detector counts **every**
re-issue of an intervened signature as starvation, but a spec-driven
session's healthy loop is run → see census → *edit* → re-run. A re-run
*after an edit* is verification, not starvation; a re-run *without* one
(the round-1 slicer flail) is the pathology. Reflex v1 cannot tell them
apart — it never sees Edit/Write. So part of the ledger is
false-positive, densify latched earlier than needed (harmless to
correctness, noisy to the slow loop), and the true remaining gap between
21–25 turns and naive's 11–13 is the ordinary edit-verify cadence plus
harness round overhead, not a re-run spiral.

**v2 refinement (filed):** starvation requires no intervening Edit/Write
touching the tested paths between the digest and the re-run — the hook
already intercepts Edit/Write; one re-arm flag per signature. That also
cleans `[digest_density]`'s training data (edit-cycle re-runs must not
promote density).

## Round 3 — reflex v2 (edit-aware starvation), and the variance wall

| task | arm | turns | cost | cache hit | holdout |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| tokenbucket | sj | 33 (cap) | $0.335 | **98.2%** | 9/9 |

| csvq | sj | 33 (cap) | $0.340 | **98.2%** | 7/7 |
| csvq | headroom | 14 | $0.117 | 90.0% | 7/7 |

Three findings, in order of importance:

1. **The variance wall.** Across three rounds of *identical* naive code,
   naive-tokenbucket swung 26 → 13 → 19 turns and naive-csvq 11 → 11 →
   19. The round-to-round swing of the BASELINE exceeds the effect the
   ≤1.5× gate tries to resolve. Single-seed cells cannot adjudicate this
   gate; every per-round sj delta we reported (33→25→33) is confounded
   with seed luck. The referee needs n≥3 paired seeds per cell before
   another mechanism iteration is judged by it.
2. **The behavior DID converge — the ledger is now trustworthy.** The r3
   csvq-sj transcript: 17 Edit/Write calls, 10 Bash calls — editing
   cadence, not the r1 slicer flail (25 pytest-slicer calls, 0 edits in
   the loop). v2's ledger recorded starvation only for genuine no-edit
   re-run bursts (2 events among ~7 single-test runs, the rest correctly
   disarmed by interleaved edits). The closed loop's *instrument* is now
   clean enough to train `[digest_density]` on.
3. **The residual gap is structural, and it is the documented regime.**
   This seed's sessions struggled with a real spec corner
   (`numeric_vs_string` comparison) in both arms; sj adds per-round
   harness overhead to every extra iteration. Small spec-driven creation
   is the "tiny surgical tasks: parity is the ceiling" regime from the
   matrix evals — and the honest lever there is not a sharper reflex but
   **graduated steering**: don't route test runs through the birth gate
   at all until the session's measured scale proves containment pays
   (the engagement ladder applied to steering strength — LADDERS §5
   candidate, now with three rounds of motivation).

**Where the referee loop lands:** reflex v1 killed the pathological loop
(r1: 8× same-command flail → r2/r3: none); v2 made the detector honest
(edit-verify cadence no longer scores); the census made pytest digests
structurally complete. What remains is (a) statistics — the gate itself
needs n≥3 to mean anything, and (b) a regime decision — steering
strength graduated by measured task scale. Both are filed; neither is a
reflex iteration.

## Verdict

On spec-driven creation at this scale with haiku: **naive and headroom
beat straitjacket on cost/turns/wall-clock; straitjacket wins cache
economics; quality ties.** The loss is localized to one diagnosable loop
(incomplete pytest census × untaught retrieval), both halves of which are
existing, ranked mechanism candidates. Re-run this exact benchmark after
fix 1 lands — the runner and holdout suites are frozen in
`evals/spec3_runner.py` for that purpose.

## ALGEBRA live A/B (n=1 each, haiku): honest negative, precise diagnosis

Task: classify 3 failures as change-introduced vs pre-existing, fix only
the introduced. Untaught arm: 20 turns / $0.122 / 58s — CORRECT (both
lines + right fixes). Taught arm (`ctx q 'fails last | in-changed'`
one-liner): 26 turns / $0.182 — right fixes, format lines missed at cap.

wins (1:8.7 authored tokens, 22.3x join compression) did not translate
at n=1 — precondition opacity converted a one-digest answer into four
dry attempts. The defect class is the session's oldest lesson recurring:
an affordance whose empty result teaches nothing converts to
re-execution. Fix filed: `fails last` with no derivable run must either
auto-capture under the birth gate or teach the missing precondition in
its empty-result digest. n>=3 taught-vs-untaught referee required before
any doctrine claim.
